ABOUT NEXUS

NEXUS // CYBEROPS

NEXUS is a browser-based cybersecurity workstation designed for defensive security learning, digital forensics and controlled experimentation.

The architecture prioritizes local processing wherever possible. Sensitive files and tokens processed by the current modules remain inside the browser.
